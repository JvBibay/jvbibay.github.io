About me

I'm an Odoo developer with more than two years of experience working on People Navee X, a Philippine HR management system built on Odoo. My work sits in the modules that HR teams rely on every day: attendance and timekeeping, payroll, leave and employee management.

I hold a BSIT from the University of Pangasinan, where I studied Data Structures and Algorithms, Operating Systems, Database Management Systems and Web Development. That foundation helps me write code that is correct, maintainable and easy for teammates to pick up.
